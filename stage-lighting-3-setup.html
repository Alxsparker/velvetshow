Set up

What to click, in which order, in each app. This part is only an outline.

In the order of a show

One app after another

The exact settings of each screen are not verified yet. Below: what is already certain and what is left to write.

Step · 1

Velvet Visualizer

Already known — prepare

  • It works with no gear: a 3D preview on the Mac.
  • To see the box's real light, turn on the Live DMX switch (green light).
  • The Mac and the box must be on the same network.

Still to write

  • The exact path on screen to confirm.
  • How to import the box's fixtures to confirm.

Step · 2

EzQuiel

Already known — install

  • iPad and iPhone app on the App Store, to set up the sound on site.
  • Made for Allen & Heath CQ mixers.

Still to write

  • Connecting the iPad to the mixer and setting the sound to confirm.
  • The link with lighting: making sure the mixer sends sound to the box to confirm.

Step · 3

Velvet Show

Already known — live

  • It imports the cues created in MaestroDMX and fires them (MIDI or OSC) at the right moment in the track.
  • Velvet Remote (iPhone, iPad) works as a remote.

Still to write

  • Importing the cues, placing them on the timeline to confirm.
  • Setting the OSC output (box address and port) to confirm.

Step · 4

Velvet Light DJ

Already known — autopilot

  • Made for MaestroDMX only.
  • It sends a cue number to the box over the network (OSC, adjustable port) depending on the track started.
  • “Auto lights” starts paused: nothing is sent until you turn it on.

Still to write

  • Reading the box's cues, correcting a choice by hand to confirm.
  • Setting the OSC port to confirm.
