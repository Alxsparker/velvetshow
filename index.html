FAQ

Before you download.

Is $79 really a one-time purchase?

Yes. $79 is a one-time licence, not a subscription. There's a 30-day free trial before you pay anything.

Why isn't Velvet Show on the Mac App Store?

Velvet Show is distributed directly from GitHub Releases rather than the Mac App Store, downloaded as a standard macOS app. Velvet Remote for iPhone and iPad is on the App Store.

Do I need Velvet Remote to use Velvet Show?

No. Velvet Show runs a complete show from the Mac on its own. Velvet Remote is a free companion app for iPhone and iPad — install it when you want a wireless prompter or a remote control on stage.

Can I move my setlists over from ShowBuddy, Stage Traxx or BandHelper?

There's no automatic importer yet. Your audio files work as-is — rebuilding a setlist in Velvet Show is a manual, one-time setup, usually a couple of hours for a full repertoire.

Does Velvet Show need an internet connection on stage?

No. Velvet Show and Velvet Remote run fully offline — Velvet Remote connects to Velvet Show over your local WiFi via Bonjour, with no cloud dependency.

Can I use MIDI and OSC at the same time?

Yes. Velvet Show can send MIDI and OSC cues simultaneously from the same timeline.

What if I need help or want a refund?

Use the 30-day free trial to run a real show with it before buying. If you have questions before or after, contact us directly — see the form below.
