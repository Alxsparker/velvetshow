Support — Velvet Visualizer for iPad

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Need a hand? Start with the sections below, or write to us: we answer by email.

Getting started

  • Open the app: on first launch you land on a demo stage with the light show running. Tap the play button on the left to start or stop the show, the moon button to switch between day and night view.
  • Tap Add a fixture in the side panel to place a light. Drag it to move it, drag the green ball to raise or lower it, use two fingers near it to rotate it.
  • The … menu in the side panel lets you create a new scene, open a .velvet file made on Velvet Visualizer for Mac, import a fixture file (.gdtf) and send a scene.

Receive the signal from your console

  • Put the iPad and your console or computer on the same Wi-Fi or wired network.
  • Art-Net: send to the iPad's IP address (or broadcast) on UDP port 6454. Art-Net universe 0 is universe 1 in the app.
  • sACN (E1.31): send to the iPad's IP address (unicast) on UDP port 5568. The app listens to universes 1 to 8. Unicast is the most reliable; multicast depends on your network.
  • Find the iPad's address in Settings → Wi-Fi → (i) next to your network.
  • The status line at the top left shows signal received when data arrives.
  • Make sure each fixture's universe, DMX address and mode in the app match your console's patch.
  • If iPadOS asked for access to the local network and you refused, enable it in Settings → Velvet Visualizer → Local Network.

Free version and unlock

  • The free version lets you build scenes with up to 6 fixtures per scene and keep 3 scenes. A one-time purchase removes the limits and adds the online catalogue (over 10,000 fixtures), fixture file import and file sending. No subscription.
  • Restore Purchases: if you already bought on another iPad with the same Apple Account, open the … menu and choose Restore Purchases.

Files and the online catalogue

  • Your scenes are .velvet files, the same format as Velvet Visualizer for Mac. They are in the app's folder, visible in the Files app.
  • Fixtures from the online catalogue are downloaded once, then stay on the iPad and work without a connection.
  • If a download fails, check your Internet connection and try again.

Requirements

iPad with iPadOS 17 or later. A lighting console, a computer or an interface that sends Art-Net or sACN is needed only to drive the lights live; the app works without one.
