Situation 1

I am a DJ with a Mac

You mix with djay on a Mac. You want the lights to follow your tracks without anyone pressing a button.

The diagram, top to bottom

  1. The Mac plays the music with djay. The sound goes to the mixer to confirm.
  2. The mixer sends the sound to the box's line input. That is how the box “hears” to confirm.
  3. Velvet Light DJ sees the track you start in djay, picks one of your cues, and sends its number to the box over the network (OSC message).
  4. The box plays the cue and sends the light as DMX to the fixtures, and as Art-Net, over the network, to the Mac.
  5. Velvet Visualizer shows that light in 3D if the Live DMX switch is on (green light). Velvet Light DJ also receives the colours.

What you need

  • A Mac, with djay and Velvet Light DJ.
  • A MaestroDMX box, with your cues already created in it.
  • A mixer that is on and sends sound to the box.
  • The Mac and the box on the same network.

What this diagram does not say

  • It does not work with another box: Velvet Light DJ is made for MaestroDMX only.
  • It does not make up music: with no sound on the line input, the beams stay frozen.

Details still to confirm

  • How the Mac's sound reaches the mixer, and which cable or adapter to use.
  • Which mixer output goes to the box's line input, and with which cable.
  • The router: which one, and how the Mac, the box and the fixtures connect to it (network cable or Wi-Fi).
  • How the fixtures receive the box's light, and their power supply.

Situation 2

I am a band with backing tracks

Diagram to come

Velvet Show plays your backing tracks or clicks and, at the right moment in the track, fires the box's cues (MIDI or OSC). Velvet Remote, on iPhone or iPad, works as a remote.

  • Cues are created in MaestroDMX; Velvet Show imports them.
  • Mixer on: the box listens only to its line input.
  • To draw: the band's sound path, the CQ mixer, the earpieces to confirm.

Situation 3

I am preparing an installation

Diagram to come

You are preparing a venue or a show before going on site. Velvet Visualizer shows your lighting in 3D, with no gear at all.

  • Nothing to plug in for the preview: a Mac is enough.
  • With the box, you add the shared network, then the Live DMX switch.
  • To draw: the typical plan of a fixed installation to confirm.
