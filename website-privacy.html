Website privacy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This website sets no cookies, shows no ads, has no account and loads no third-party trackers.

Who is responsible

Alexandre Chalon (see the legal notice). Contact: contact@loveandlive.fr.

When you visit

The site is hosted by GitHub (GitHub Pages). Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address and technical details of your browser to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running and securing the site.

Visit counter

When a page opens, your browser sends to our counter (a database run by Supabase): the page address (for example /velvet-visualizer.html), an optional source tag if the link you clicked had one (for example ?s=soundswitch), the name of the site you came from (the site name only, never the full address), and the time. For download buttons it also records which app was clicked.

We store only these items. We store no IP address, no cookie, no identifier and no fingerprint, so a visit cannot be traced back to you. The platform itself may keep short-term technical logs. Legal basis: legitimate interest in knowing which pages are read, without profiling.

Opt out: use the button below. It stores a small flag in your browser (local storage) so that your visits are no longer counted.

Download form (Velvet Visualizer, Velvet Show, Velvet Light DJ trials)

Before the free trial of Velvet Visualizer, Velvet Show or Velvet Light DJ downloads, a window asks for your email address. It is sent to a small program hosted at Cloudflare, which stores it at Resend, our email-sending service. If that service is down, the download starts anyway.

What we receive: your email address, the language of the page, and whether you ticked the box. The program does not store your IP address; it uses it for a moment to limit abuse, and Cloudflare may keep short-lived technical logs.

  • Box not ticked: your address is stored as “unsubscribed”. You receive no message from us, except a reply if you wrote to us. We use it to know how many people try our apps. Legal basis: legitimate interest. These addresses are deleted after 12 months.
  • Box ticked: we first send you a confirmation email. You are subscribed only if you click the button in that email. You then receive three emails during the trial (the first right away, then after 7 days and after 25 days) about the app you downloaded and the other Velvet apps (one series per app; we remember which one has started so we do not send it twice). Legal basis: your consent. Your address is kept until you unsubscribe.

Every email contains an unsubscribe link. You can also ask for your address to be erased by writing to contact@loveandlive.fr. Resend is a US company, so your data may be processed outside the European Union. See the privacy policies of Resend and Cloudflare.

Fonts and images

The fonts and images are served from this site. Your browser does not contact Google or any other font service.

Links to other sites

Buying (Lemon Squeezy) and downloading (GitHub) take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

None. Your browser only keeps, if you use them: the opt-out flag above; and, for the length of your visit only (erased when you close the tab), a flag showing where you came from and another showing you already filled in the download form.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask for access, correction or deletion of your data, or object to its processing: write to contact@loveandlive.fr. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL, cnil.fr).

The apps

The apps have their own policies: Velvet Show, Velvet Remote and Velvet Light DJ, EzQuiel.
