Stage Traxx Alternative

The Stage Traxx alternative built for modern live shows

Stage Traxx 4 is a backing track and setlist app for iPad, iPhone and Mac, with lyrics, MIDI automation and network sessions. Velvet Show is a macOS alternative built around OSC and MaestroDMX: its cues sit on the same timeline as the audio.

How Velvet Show differs from Stage Traxx

Both apps handle backing tracks, setlists, lyrics and MIDI. Velvet Show puts MIDI and OSC cues directly on the audio timeline and talks to MaestroDMX natively, so lights and visuals can fire without a second operator. Stage Traxx’s website does not list OSC or MaestroDMX support.

  • MIDI timeline cues — lighting scenes fire exactly when the music does
  • OSC engine — works with MaestroDMX and any network device
  • Velvet Remote — iPhone and iPad control over local WiFi
  • Lyrics and chords synced to the waveform
  • Stage screen output for iPad or second monitor
  • Color-coded song grid for fast navigation on stage
  • PANIC mode — an instant backup prompter, always one keystroke away

Where Stage Traxx fits better

Stage Traxx is built for iPad and iPhone (iOS 16.6 or later, and macOS 13.6 or later), with up to 32 tracks per song and 16 output buses. It is free for up to 6 songs, then a one-time $29.99 purchase. If you work mainly on a tablet, it is a solid choice.

Who is this for?

Velvet Show suits you if you play weddings, corporate events or live shows and want to run lighting, OSC and MaestroDMX from the same app on a Mac, with an iPhone remote. If you work mainly on an iPad or iPhone, Stage Traxx is built for that.

Getting started

Download the macOS build and run the guided demo show. No account, no App Store, no subscription. Your existing audio files work immediately.
