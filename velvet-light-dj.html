How it works

Three steps,
nothing to program.

The app reads djay's own history, read-only. Nothing is ever written into djay, and nothing changes in the way you DJ.

  • Reads the history 5 times per second — djay logs each track the second it starts.
  • Cues leave 300 ms early — the same lead as Velvet Show, so lights land on the beat.
  • One switch to stop everything — "Auto lights" can be paused instantly, and starts paused.
  • Fix any track by hand — your choice is remembered for next time.

Full pipeline

djay → history Velvet Light DJ → OSC MaestroDMX → Art-Net DMX fixtures

Live colours

The app window takes the colours of your real fixtures in real time (flowers, towers, beams), received over Art-Net from the MaestroDMX box. A pinnable mini window stays on top, even over djay full screen.

Which cue, and why

It picks, it explains,
it learns.

For every track the app proposes its three best cues and says why. You always have the last word.

  • Rule first — the genre written in the file, then the folder name, then the title.
  • Then what it learned — careful learning: same cue for 2 tracks of an artist, or 3 of a genre, with no contrary choice.
  • Then energy — from the cue's own settings, and finally the tempo.
  • Peak cue on the first real drop — a bass rise of at least 12 dB, never on calm moods.
  • Works with any show — it guesses 23 track styles from your cue names and energy, and asks you to confirm.

Priority order

1 · File genre→ 2 · Folder→ 3 · Title→ 4 · Tempo

Your manual choices and what the app learned always come before the automatic guess.

Honest limits

What it doesn't do yet.

  • If you change a track's pitch in djay, the drop lands a bit before or after the cue.
  • If you jump inside a track, the peak cue keeps its planned time.
  • Streamed tracks (Apple Music…) have no file to analyse: the mood follows the tempo, with no peak cue.
  • The MaestroDMX box only reacts to its line input: if the mixer is off, beams stay still even if the cues are right.
  • VirtualDJ is partly working and still being tested. Serato and rekordbox are not supported yet.
