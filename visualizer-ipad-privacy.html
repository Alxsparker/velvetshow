Privacy Policy — Velvet Visualizer for iPad

Last updated: November 17, 2026

In short: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your scenes stay on your iPad. The app uses the Internet only to download fixture files from the online catalogue when you ask for them.

What stays on your iPad

  • Your scenes (.velvet files) and the fixture files you import are stored only in the app's folder on your iPad (visible in the Files app). They are never sent anywhere, unless you choose to share a file yourself (AirDrop, Files, Mail).
  • Your settings (last scene opened, panel position, whether the purchase was made) are stored on the iPad.
  • Local network. The app uses your local network only to receive the Art-Net and sACN lighting signals sent by your console or computer. These signals stay on your network and are not recorded.

What goes over the Internet

  • Online fixture catalogue. When you open the online catalogue, or pick a fixture that is not yet on your iPad, the app downloads the fixture index and the file you asked for from velvet-gdtf.pages.dev (a server hosted by Cloudflare). The files are manufacturers' fixture descriptions published on GDTF Share. As with any website, that server sees your IP address and the app's name, and its host may keep them in technical logs. The app sends nothing else about you: no name, no identifier, no scene.
  • Purchase. The one-time unlock goes through Apple's App Store. We never see your card or your identity; the app only learns from Apple that the purchase was made.

What there isn't

  • No account, no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no third-party analytics or trackers.
  • No selling or sharing of data.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you. Deleting the app erases everything it stored on your iPad. If you have a question about this policy, write to the address below. In the EU you can also contact your data protection authority.
