Beginner guide

Stage lighting
in 5 minutes

Never touched stage lighting? This guide explains the words, shows what plugs into what, then what to click. No prior knowledge needed.

In the order of a show

Four steps, four tools

Each tool picks up where the previous one stops. You can use just one, or all four.

1

Velvet Visualizer

Prepare

You see your lighting in 3D on screen, with no gear at all. The place to try, get it wrong and understand.

2

EzQuiel

Install

An iPad and iPhone app (on the App Store) to set up the sound on site, with Allen & Heath CQ mixers.

3

Velvet Show

Live

Your band plays with backing tracks or clicks. A timeline fires the sound and the lights at the right moment.

4

Velvet Light DJ

On autopilot

For a DJ: the app picks and sends the cues by itself, depending on the tracks you play. MaestroDMX only.
