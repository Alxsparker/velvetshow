Plugged into real hardware

What the console sends
shows up in 3D.

Live DMX reception via Art-Net and sACN — whatever your lighting software sends appears on the 3D stage, fixture by fixture.

  • Art-Net and sACN — WiFi or Ethernet
  • Compatible with MaestroDMX, SoundSwitch, QLC+, QLab and any Art-Net software
  • Direct MaestroDMX patch import — no need to redo everything by hand
  • Record a DMX snapshot and replay it without hardware
  • DMX monitor — channel-by-channel values live
  • Built-in Art-Net assistant if you're not receiving anything
  • Visible from your console — Velvet answers Art-Net discovery (ArtPoll), so it shows up in SoundSwitch, QLC+ or Lightkey's device list

Fixture library

Your fixture
is probably there.

645 real fixtures are built in, with their real dimensions, no Internet needed. For a specific fixture, import its official GDTF file. And if nothing exists, build it yourself.

  • 645 real fixtures built in — Open Fixture Library, offline: type “gigbar” or “martin mac”, click Add, it's on stage
  • PAR, moving heads, wash, beam, LED bars, pixel tubes, GigBAR — Chauvet, ADJ, Martin, Cameo, BeamZ, Astera...
  • GDTF import — official spec files with online GDTF Share search
  • Custom fixture builder — describe channels once, available in all projects
  • Gobos and prisms
  • LED screens — image, animated GIF, looping video, or one of 8 built-in animations that move with the music
  • Effects — cold spark machines and low fog, triggered by DMX or a button
  • Aim at a point — click on the floor or an object, the moving head aims
